Know the limits

A stable camera reading is not a calibrated measurement. Lumen cannot establish an absolute white point, brightness in cd/m2, gamut coverage or colour accuracy. Use a suitable measurement instrument for colour-critical work.

Get started
  1. Open the monitor page over HTTPS and enter fullscreen. Keep the display settings fixed; turn off HDR, dynamic contrast and adaptive brightness.
  2. Scan the QR code, use the pairing link or enter the five-character code on the phone page.
  3. Choose a target and Quick, Standard, Detailed or Full Test. Start the camera, then match the monitor square to the square in the phone preview. Rest the phone on a support.
  4. Tap Lock sampling. Exposure and clipping checks must pass before Run is available. Perfect square alignment is not required; a manual lock is labelled as unconfirmed.
  5. Run the selected test. Keep both pages and the phone preview visible. Both devices show progress. A pause or disconnect preserves readings; realign and revalidate before resuming.

Why two patches?

A test patch is compared with a reference in the same camera frame. A shared multiplicative gain can cancel in that ratio. A ladder of reference levels reduces the brightness gap for darker patches.

That model does not remove local tone mapping, unknown camera colour response, lens shading or browser colour management. Lumen checks sample stability, clipping and reference completeness, but passing those checks cannot prove physical accuracy.

Your sessions, under your control

Camera frames are processed on the phone; only numerical readings and control messages are sent to the paired display. Checkpoints are saved in IndexedDB with a localStorage fallback on both devices when available. Cookies are not used. Export JSON for a portable copy and use Clear saved data on the monitor to remove its browser copy.

The app loads PeerJS and a QR library from public CDNs, and pairing uses a public signalling service. It is not an offline or zero-network tool. Share the pairing code only with the phone you intend to use.
