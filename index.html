A useful experiment. Not a certified calibration.

Lumen checks relative camera response, not absolute brightness, white point, gamut coverage or calibrated colour accuracy. A quality rating describes observed repeatability, not physical accuracy. Use a suitable measurement instrument for colour-critical work.

ICC/CAL downloads require complete clean readings and camera-confirmed exposure and white-balance locks. Safari may not expose those controls, so diagnostics can be available while correction exports remain blocked.

From pairing to a report
  1. Open the monitor page over HTTPS. Use your final monitor settings. Disable HDR, adaptive brightness and colour-shifting modes. Enter fullscreen before alignment.
  2. Open the pairing link in iPhone Safari, scan the QR code or enter the five-character code. Both pages must show v2.1.0.
  3. Choose your target and test depth. Start the rear camera and rest the phone on a support.
  4. Match the monitor square with the dashed phone guide. Both sampling dots must sit inside the two patches. Automatic guidance helps; imperfect alignment does not block Lock.
  5. Tap Lock sampling & check exposure. Resolve clipping before starting. Camera lock limitations are reported honestly.
  6. Run the test. The monitor square and markers stay in place while the patches change. Keep the phone still and both pages visible.
  7. Review the complete report. Unavailable sections say Not measured. Save JSON for a portable session, CSV for raw readings or HTML for a readable report.
Recovery, not a silent spinner

A failed sample retries twice, then pauses with a reason on both screens. Marker-detected movement also pauses the test. Manual alignment cannot provide automatic movement protection.

Completed readings are checkpointed on both devices. Resume requires realignment, a camera reference recheck and confirmation that monitor settings are unchanged. Browser storage is not a backup: export JSON before closing an important session.

Data stays local to your session

Camera images are processed on the iPhone and are not sent to the monitor or saved in session files. Numerical readings and control messages go to the paired monitor. IndexedDB stores checkpoints, with a localStorage fallback. No account or cookies are required.

Pairing uses PeerJS, public signalling and WebRTC. PeerJS and the QR library load from public CDNs, so an internet connection is required. Share the pairing code only with the phone you intend to connect.
